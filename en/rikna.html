What is Rikna

Rikna is a Serbian app for readers. It's built around one simple thing: knowing what you've read and when, without keeping notes in notebooks and apps that aren't made for it.

01

Your shelf

Every book goes on one of three rows — Reading, Read or Wishlist. A finished book gets a rating and a review, as short as you like. You can bring your shelf over from another app; a CSV export is all it takes.

02

A habit you can see

The calendar marks every day you read. Your streak grows, a yearly goal shows where you stand, and 24 badges arrive as you go — for your first book, for morning reading, for reaching your goal.

03

Community

The feed shows reviews from people you follow. Reviews can be commented on and liked, and booktubers can add a link to their video. Authors and publishers have profiles with their titles, announce launches and signings, and post deals.

04

Catalog

Over 24,000 books in Serbian, with covers, descriptions, year and page count. If a book is missing, scan the barcode on the back cover or add it by hand.
